Orbit Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Applies to the Orbit Rush app for Android.

Orbit Rush is published by [ETS HOLDINGS PTY LTD] (ABN [ABN]), [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. Orbit Rush has no accounts, logins or sign-up. Your scores, coins, unlocked worlds and settings (such as music on or off) are saved only on your device. We do not receive them, and they are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one. Occasional interstitial and banner ads may also appear unless you buy Remove Ads.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to personalise ads where you have allowed it. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent or gives you an opt-out before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy & ad options in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs and Remove Ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment. We never see your card or payment details. We receive only a purchase confirmation, used to deliver what you bought and to restore Remove Ads.

Crash and diagnostic information

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports and performance statistics if you have enabled this in your Android settings. These do not identify you.

Children

Orbit Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with Google only as described above, so that ads and purchases work.

Security and retention

Because game data stays on your device, we hold no copy of it. Data collected by Google is handled under Google's own retention and security practices.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. As we hold no personal information about you, most requests are best directed to Google, but you can always contact us and we will help.

Changes

If we change this policy, for example if we add analytics, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
